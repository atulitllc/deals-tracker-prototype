How to Cancel Draftly (Web, iPhone, Android)

Cancel at least a day before renewal so you are not charged.

Web

  1. Sign in at the Draftly website.
  2. Open Account, then Subscription.
  3. Choose Cancel plan and confirm.

iPhone

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Draftly.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Android

  1. Open the Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions.
  3. Pick Draftly and tap Cancel.
