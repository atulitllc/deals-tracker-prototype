How to cancel Spotify Premium

Web

  1. Log in to your account page.
  2. Go to Manage your plan.
  3. Select Cancel subscription.

Spotify says that if you cancel during a $0 free trial, your account switches to the free service right away, and a $0 trial cannot be reactivated.
