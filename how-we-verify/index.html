How We Verify

We open each service's official US plans or signup page and record only what it states: trial length, price after the trial and any conditions. Cancel steps come from the service's official help page. Each entry shows a source link and the date checked (Oct 13, 2026).

Where the official page does not state something, we write Not shown. We do not publish ways to get around trial limits.

Not done yet

We have not yet verified card requirements, regions, or cancel steps for every service. Those fields are left out until verified.
