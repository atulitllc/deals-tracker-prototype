How We Verify

  1. We open the official signup page from a US connection.
  2. We record trial length, card requirement, region and renewal terms.
  3. We stamp a Last checked date and log any change in the history.
  4. Expired deals are marked with the date and alternatives.

We do not publish ways to get around trial limits.
