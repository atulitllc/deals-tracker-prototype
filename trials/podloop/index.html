How to cancel PodLoop

Web

  1. Sign in at the PodLoop website.
  2. Open Account, then Subscription.
  3. Choose Cancel plan and confirm.

iPhone

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then PodLoop.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Android

  1. Open the Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions.
  3. Pick PodLoop and tap Cancel.
