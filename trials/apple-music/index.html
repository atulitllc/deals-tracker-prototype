How to cancel Apple Music

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap the subscription, then tap Cancel Subscription. If there is no Cancel button, it is already canceled.

Mac

  1. Open the App Store and click your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Click Account Settings, then find Subscriptions and click Manage.
  3. Click Edit next to the subscription, then Cancel Subscription.
